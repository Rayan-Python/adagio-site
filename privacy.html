Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

The short version

  • Your runs are stored in your own private iCloud, not on servers we run. We can't see them.
  • Only a short summary of each run is synced. Raw Apple Health data and GPS routes stay on your phone.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for ads.
  • This website counts visits without cookies and doesn't know who you are.

Who we are

Adagio is a running app for iPhone, Apple Watch and Garmin, made by Ryan Soudkhah and Darren Kapturski. "We" in this policy means the Adagio team. You can reach us at adagiorunning.support@gmail.com.

The Adagio app

What the app reads

To match music to your running, the app reads your cadence (steps per minute) during a run. On iPhone and Apple Watch runs, cadence comes from Apple Health (HealthKit), with your permission. Garmin runs send their data through the app on your iPhone.

Your run history

The app keeps your run history on your phone. After each run, it also saves a summary of the run to your private iCloud database, so you can see it on the My runs page. Runs you did before this feature are uploaded once, the same way. The summary contains:

  • the date and time, duration, and distance (when available)
  • your average cadence and your cadence over the run, as one reading about every 5 seconds
  • your target cadence (for single-target runs)
  • the songs that played, as "Title — Artist"
  • whether Pulse Mode was on
  • whether the run came from iPhone, Apple Watch or Garmin

No raw Apple Health data, GPS route, or location is uploaded.

Where it's stored

Run summaries are stored with Apple's CloudKit, in the private database of your own iCloud account. Apple's privacy policy covers how iCloud stores data. Private iCloud data is only available to you when you're signed in with your Apple ID: we, the developers, can't read it.

Music services

Adagio connects to Spotify and Apple Music to play songs that match your cadence. Your use of those services is covered by their own privacy policies.

The My runs page

The My runs page on adagiorunning.com lets you see your run summaries in a web browser. You sign in with your Apple ID through Apple. We never see your password. The page reads your runs directly from iCloud in your browser: they don't pass through our servers, and we don't keep a copy. The page has no analytics.

This website

The website is hosted by Vercel. We use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to count page visits and measure how fast pages load. Vercel says these tools don't use cookies and don't identify individual visitors. Like most web hosts, Vercel may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

There are no accounts on the main website and no forms that collect personal information.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't use your health or run data for advertising or marketing.
  • We don't share your data with data brokers.

Deleting your data

  • Runs on your phone: delete them in the app, or delete the app.
  • Runs in iCloud: you can delete Adagio's iCloud data from your iPhone's iCloud storage settings.
  • Questions or help: email us at adagiorunning.support@gmail.com.

Security

Your runs are protected by your Apple ID and Apple's iCloud security. The website is served only over HTTPS and uses strict browser security settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For bigger changes, we'll also say so in the app.

Contact

Email adagiorunning.support@gmail.com with any questions about your privacy.
